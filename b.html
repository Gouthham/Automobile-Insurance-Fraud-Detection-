What is Car Insurance?

Once you’ve secured the car of your dreams, the next step is to get car insurance from a reliable provider. Think of it as a safety net for your beloved ride and the loved ones who will use it. Car insurance is a contract between an insurer and you to protect you from accidents, theft, or natural calamities.

According to the Motor Vehicles Act of 1988, every car owner must have at least a third-party insurance policy. You could also choose comprehensive insurance plans providing wider protection.

Today, getting car insurance or renewing it has become very easy. You can find many online options, but we help you find the best value without breaking your bank.

Key Features of a Car Insurance Policy

  • Financial Protection Against Damages: Covers repair costs from accidents, theft, or disasters.
  • Third-Party Liability Coverage: Mandatory coverage for injury or property damage to others.
  • Personal Accident Cover: Financial support if the owner-driver faces injury or death.
  • No Claim Bonus (NCB): Save money if no claims are made during the policy term.
  • Cashless Repairs: Repairs at network garages without upfront payment.
